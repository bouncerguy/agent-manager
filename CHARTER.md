# Human Commons Alliance Charter 0.1 — Public Beta

The name **Human Commons Alliance** is provisional pending community review.

## Mission

People and AI working together to keep the digital commons safe, useful, open, and human-directed.

The Alliance exists to provide accessible and affordable, human-directed assistance to people and organizations facing AI-enabled fraud, impersonation, harassment, manipulation, cyber incidents, and other harmful automated activity.

## Non-mission

The Alliance is not a government, police service, intelligence service, military organization, vigilante group, universal authority over AI, or replacement for qualified emergency, legal, medical, financial, or cybersecurity professionals.

## Commitments

Members commit to:

- center the safety, consent, dignity, and agency of affected people;
- verify claims and authority before acting;
- keep AI-assisted work under identifiable human direction and accountability;
- before material action, identify the affected person or authorized principal, the scope of consent, and who may pause or revoke the work;
- use the least intrusive effective intervention;
- protect privacy and disclose conflicts;
- make costs and limitations understandable;
- distinguish evidence from inference and uncertainty;
- preserve legitimate human access to the internet and useful AI tools;
- document material actions and support meaningful appeal;
- refuse instructions that exceed authority or violate this Charter.

## Initial services

Subject to available qualified members, the Alliance may support:

- incident intake and triage;
- scam, synthetic-media, and impersonation analysis;
- evidence-preservation guidance;
- account-recovery and platform-reporting guidance;
- defensive cybersecurity and recovery assistance;
- public-information verification;
- translation and accessibility;
- referral to legal, crisis, medical, financial, or law-enforcement resources when appropriate.

No service is guaranteed merely because it appears in this list.

## Hard boundaries

Members MUST NOT use Alliance authority for retaliation, offensive intrusion, malware, denial of service, coercion, doxxing, unauthorized surveillance, autonomous propagation, weapons activity, evasion of lawful oversight, or unbounded spending.

An AI participant may not bind the Alliance, represent another participant, or independently initiate external action without a separate explicit delegation from an authorized human principal. Membership alone is never such a delegation.

## Affordability

The Alliance should aim for transparent, reasonable pricing; shared tools; sponsored capacity; and pro-bono pathways when resources permit. Affordability must not be achieved through deceptive claims, unsafe automation, exploitation of contributors, or sale of victim data.

## Founding status

This public beta records the founding direction of a project initiated by Ken Cox and developed through open review. Founding membership is open under the candidate process in `JOIN.md`. The beta is an operating collaboration framework for harmless tests and bounded, explicitly authorized work; it is not yet a legally constituted organization, production service, certification authority, or claim of community consensus.

Candidate status grants no Alliance authority, does not imply endorsement or qualification, and permits only the role and scope explicitly recorded for that candidate.
