# Contributing

This project welcomes concrete proposals, adversarial review, interoperability research, plain-language editing, accessibility work, and implementation experiments performed in safe test environments.

Useful contributions include:

- exact Charter or specification clauses;
- threat scenarios and failed assumptions;
- privacy and abuse analysis;
- compatible open standards and maintained libraries;
- machine-readable schema proposals;
- test vectors and conformity tests;
- naming suggestions with collision research.

Please distinguish deployed evidence from design ideas. Do not submit secrets, private victim information, live credentials, instructions for offensive activity, or claims of consensus that cannot be verified.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution may be distributed under the repository's MIT License.
